Can a face reveal your politics?

      
./verdict --subject=faces --target=political_orientation

      
The answer from ten years of Stanford research: a weak signal across millions of faces, close to a coin flip for any one person.

      01 · is it possible?
      
Groups: barely. One person: no.

      
Michal Kosinski at Stanford GSB trained face-recognition models to separate liberals from conservatives. Across large samples the models beat chance, so faces carry some information. Once expression, pose and grooming are controlled, that information explains about 5% of the difference between people.

      
Kosinski presents his results as a privacy warning, not as a working tool.

      04 · properties
      
What the models pick up

    

    

      
Head poseIn 2021, liberals more often faced the camera directly.

      
ExpressionIn 2021, liberals showed more surprise and less disgust.

      
Lower faceIn 2024, conservatives tended to have a larger lower face. This was the only structural difference reported.

      
GroomingBeards, glasses, makeup and hair follow subcultures. They drive much of the signal in everyday photos.

      
DemographicsAge, gender and ethnicity correlate with politics. Adding them raised the 2024 result from r = .22 to .31.

      
Image itselfLighting, filters and platform style. None of this is biology.

      05 · personality & sexual orientation
      
The same pattern for other traits

      
Studies of personality and sexual orientation from faces show the same pattern: small effects in large samples, and most of the signal comes from self-presentation.

    

    

      
Selfies → Big FiveKachur et al. 2020: 12,447 volunteers, mean r = .24. The data came from the employer of three of the five authors.

      
3D face shapeHu et al. 2017 (Shanghai, Fudan): 834 Han Chinese adults. Face shape explains 0.7–4% of personality variance.

      
Human judgesJaeger et al. 2024: personality impressions from faces are not accurate, and people overestimate their accuracy. Trustworthiness meta-analysis: r = .14.

      
Hiring and payNBER 2026: "Photo Big 5" scores from LinkedIn photos of 96,000 MBA graduates predict pay and seniority. The authors warn against using it in hiring.

      
Sexual orientationWang & Kosinski 2018: 81% / 74% pairwise. Leuner 2019: a model on blurred photos still reached 63% / 72%, so much of the signal is not facial structure.

      
Chatbots with visionJeon et al. 2026: GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini and LLaVA rated the same campaign ad as more liberal when it showed a woman. The models apply demographic stereotypes.

      06 · advertising & political profiling
      
Where it is used today

      
Profiling for advertising and politics works through digital footprints: likes, posts and browsing. Faces are used for identity and demographics. No deployed system is documented that targets political ads by face.

    

    

      
Psychological targetingMatz, Kosinski et al. (PNAS 2017): ads matched to personality inferred from Facebook Likes reached 3.5 million people and produced up to 40% more clicks and 50% more purchases.

      
Cambridge AnalyticaKogan's quiz app harvested data on up to 87 million Facebook users after Stillwell refused CA access to myPersonality (ICO 2018). The UK regulator later found CA's models used "commonly available technology" and that their real-world accuracy "was likely much lower" than claimed (ICO 2020). Facebook paid a $5 billion FTC penalty (2019).

      
Billboards & retail"Audience analytics" cameras estimate age, gender and emotion to measure or switch ads. A UK station trial in 2024 reportedly used Amazon Rekognition this way.

      
Face searchClearview AI scraped billions of web photos for police face search and was fined by several European regulators.

      
RetreatsMeta shut down Facebook face recognition in 2021 and deleted over 1 billion face templates. HireVue dropped facial analysis from hiring interviews in 2021.

      
PseudoscienceStartups such as Faception have marketed "terrorist" or "high IQ" face classifiers without published validation.

      
LawEU AI Act Art. 5(1)(g) bans inferring political opinions from biometrics. China: PIPL treats biometrics as sensitive data; the 2025 face-recognition Measures exempt R&D and algorithm training. US: Illinois BIPA, Texas CUBI, California CPRA and FTC enforcement.

      07 · who does this research
      
Companies and labs building trait classifiers

      
The face models with documented real-world use classify ethnicity, not politics. Most reported cases are Chinese surveillance vendors.

    

    

      
SenseTimeUS Treasury, 2021: software that "can determine a target's ethnicity", with a focus on Uyghurs. SenseTime called this unfounded.

      
Huawei, MegviiA 2018 test report listed a "Uyghur alert" (IPVM 2020). Huawei called it a test; Megvii said it does not label ethnic groups.

      
Hikvision, Dahua, UniviewUyghur detection in cameras, analytics and an SDK (IPVM 2019–2023).

      
Police procurementNYT 2019: one city ran 500,000 checks for Uyghur faces in a month.

      
Chinese universitiesShanghai Jiao Tong (criminality, 2016), Dalian Minzu (ethnicity, under an Expression of Concern), CAS Institute of Psychology, Fudan and NWPU Xi'an (personality).

      
ElsewhereStanford (Kosinski), Denmark (ideology from candidate photos, 61%), Russia/UK (Kachur, selfies), Israel (Faception).
